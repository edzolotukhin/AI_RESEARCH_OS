## T. Architectural interpretation

P1-07.10’s Search-fairness caveat is **falsified for this brief/run**: rank-sorted 18-attempt opportunity plus coverage early-stop achieved 12/12 source representation. The next architectural debts are not “raise Search again.”

They are:

1. **Targeted Evidence isolation** — gap extraction must not share a depleted initial-pass Evidence cap.
2. **Sufficiency incremental re-eval** — do not rescore every evidenced need on every gap round if the stage budget is the loop terminator.
3. **EvidenceExpectation / relevance** — volume without on-need excerpts cannot satisfy all-IN readiness; IN2=20 still insufficient is the proof.
4. **Low-cost remains a valid diagnostic envelope.** Profile B remains a **measurement** envelope, not a new production default.

Do not weaken `ready_for_analysis`. Do not treat CASE B as a budget defect.

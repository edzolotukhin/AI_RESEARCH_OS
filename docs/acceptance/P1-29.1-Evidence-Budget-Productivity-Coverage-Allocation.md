# P1-29.1 — Evidence Budget Productivity and Coverage Allocation

## Verdict

PROPERTY AG aligns Evidence depth allocation with observed yield and coverage while preserving the fixed 50-call Evidence cap and P1-17.1 first-opportunity fairness.

## Root cause

P1-29 established `ALLOCATION_PRIMARY`: the old scheduler prebuilt a static need-fair queue, then executed it without using completed outcomes. In P1-28.2B, 39 of 50 calls were valid-empty, nine Sources had repeated empty calls, and IMARC consumed five calls for zero Evidence. Proriat demonstrated the necessary counterconstraint: its first chunk was empty and its second was productive.

## Old architecture

1. Deduplicate eligible acquired Sources.
2. Resolve retained IN lineage and select a stable primary IN.
3. Prebuild every Source-chunk work item.
4. Execute one first chunk per Source in need-fair order.
5. Execute all remaining chunks in static need-fair round-robin order.
6. Observe outcomes only for extraction diagnostics; do not reprioritize.
7. Use an existing remediation reserve mechanism whose stock default was zero.

The scheduling unit was and remains **Source chunk × retained IN bundle**.

## New adaptive continuation

The queue inventory remains deterministic and prebuilt, but post-fairness depth selection is now made one item at a time. Run-local `SourceOutcomeState` records only executed facts:

- calls attempted;
- productive calls;
- valid-empty calls;
- newly persisted Evidence yield;
- depth calls;
- consecutive empty depth;
- last outcome;
- first-opportunity completion.

No future yield is inferred.

Depth selection uses the stable tuple:

1. repeated-zero-yield deprioritization;
2. fewest depth calls (anti-monopoly breadth);
3. most retained zero-Evidence INs;
4. observed productive before unproductive when otherwise tied;
5. primary IN;
6. Source ID;
7. chunk index.

This preserves deterministic tie-breaking and makes the next selection reconstructible.

## P1-17.1 preservation

The complete first-opportunity list is still executed before adaptive depth begins. Productivity is not considered until every budget-eligible Source has received its first opportunity. P1-17.1 is unchanged.

## Valid-empty and exploratory depth

`valid_empty_result` remains a successful structured response with no candidates—not an error, grounding failure, or provider failure.

One empty first opportunity does not suppress depth. The Source remains eligible for exploratory depth. After two observed empty calls and no productive result, subsequent depth is ranked behind non-repeated-empty alternatives. This permits Proriat's empty→productive sequence while moving IMARC-like repeated empty depth behind useful work.

A call with persisted Evidence is productive even when other candidates in that call fail grounding. PROPERTY AF and grounding semantics are unchanged.

## Coverage and concentration

Coverage uses only already-persisted Evidence counts. A depth item retaining lineage to a zero-Evidence IN gains priority when repeated-empty state and depth count are otherwise comparable. No Sufficiency call is introduced.

Fewest depth calls precedes productivity in the stable ordering, preventing a high-yield Source from monopolizing depth while peers remain eligible. Productive Sources still rank above equally deep, equally covered, nonproductive Sources.

## Remediation reserve

PROPERTY AG reuses `evidence_remediation_reserved_llm_calls`; it introduces no second budget system. The stock 50-call profile now defaults to the repository's previously accepted reserve of **6 calls**. Non-stock profiles without an explicit reserve retain zero so their established envelopes do not change.

- Total Evidence cap remains 50.
- Initial allowance becomes 44 at the Evidence partition boundary.
- Remediation may consume unused initial allowance plus the reserved slice, subject to existing total/global/downstream bounds.
- A newly acquired targeted Source receives first-opportunity treatment within remediation extraction.
- If no remediation is required, the reserve remains unused. Releasing it would require a new post-Sufficiency return-to-depth phase and is explicitly out of scope.

The live stock global downstream reserve may stop initial Evidence before call 44; the explicit six-call partition is still an independent fail-closed floor.

## Auditability

Each executed work-item trace now records:

- selection reason: `first_opportunity`, `exploratory_depth`, `productive_depth`, or `deprioritized_repeated_empty_depth`;
- Evidence budget remaining before and after;
- bounded Source outcome state before and after;
- existing phase, chunk, Source, and IN lineage.

No raw model output or secret is added.

## Offline replay conclusions

P1-28.2B and P1-27.2 recorded outcomes confirm the intended ordering:

- repeated-empty IMARC-like work ranks below productive Coherent-like depth;
- a first-empty Proriat-like Source retains one exploratory opportunity;
- first-opportunity ordering remains unchanged;
- undercovered-IN lineage participates without fabricating semantic insufficiency;
- total calls remain bounded by the existing cap.

The implementation does not claim outcomes for historically unexecuted chunks. It improves which eligible known work reaches the fixed boundary; it does not solve weak Source content.

## Explicit non-goals

- No Evidence cap or global-budget increase.
- No new provider, LLM, Sufficiency, Analysis, Report, or Review call.
- No Search, PROPERTY AC, grounding, PROPERTY AF, prompt, validator, Source→IN, semantic partition, or remediation-loop change.
- No pure productivity winner-take-all policy.
- No domain vocabulary or authority heuristic.
- No persistence migration, schema change, dependency, or durable scheduling store.

## Domain generality

Fixtures cover Ukraine pizza, New Zealand heat pumps, India UPI merchant payments, German industrial electricity, and a niche B2B industrial market. Scheduling inspects only observed outcomes, call depth, stable identity, and Evidence-count coverage.

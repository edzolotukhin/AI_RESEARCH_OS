# Non-Goals

AI Research OS is not:

- a chatbot;
- a collection of unrelated AI agents;
- an LLM wrapper;
- a prompt library;
- a workflow builder;
- a replacement for research professionals.

The system is a business operating system where AI is one of the components.

---

# Success Criteria

AI Research OS should become a digital operating system capable of supporting the daily work of a professional marketing research agency.

The platform should:

- improve project quality;
- accelerate delivery;
- preserve and reuse corporate knowledge;
- support consistent business decisions;
- keep humans in control of critical business decisions.

Every new component should make the operating system more valuable while keeping the architecture simple, maintainable, and scalable.
